- **Lake digging (France):** Continued digging the lake at the France property, begun on 28 August and completed on 11 September. Photos to follow.
- **[Reports That Matter](https://reportsthatmatter.org/changelog):** Archive grew from six reports to eleven (Columbia, 9/11 Commission, Deepwater Horizon, Philip Morris, Hillsborough), fixed the Litvinenko report's paragraphs being misread as quotations, and did a major infrastructure pass — reports stopped being committed as generated build output (133MB → 77MB), pages stopped carrying site layout so styling changes no longer require a full rebuild, and each report can now be corrected and republished independently without redeploying the whole site.
- **[Reason Commons](https://reasoncommons.com/changelog):** Redesigned the landing page and navigation, made dashboard views linkable, and shipped a reviewable contribution-proposals workflow that lets a group accept, edit, or reject proposed changes against an existing tree.
- **CRM for Life Itself:** Shipped a reusable branded signup service for courses and lists — each brand gets its own hosted signup context, with existing subscriber data kept compatible.
- **[DataHub](https://datahub.io):** Shipped two visualization bakeoff demos comparing libraries for data tables and line charts — [tables.datahub.io](https://tables.datahub.io/) and [linecharts.datahub.io](https://linecharts.datahub.io/).
- **[Marketing](https://github.com/rufuspollock/marketing/blob/main/changelog.md):** Added shared agent instructions and changelog support to the central marketing repo.

# Joe Brewer 3 part series on why he stopped working on applied cultural evolution

Reading Joe Brewer’s republished 3 part series from 2018 when he was director of the center for applied cultural evolution. In essence, it’s an explanation on why he was stopping doing the “cultural evolution” work he was doing then — or, as he phrases it in the piece, “Attempting to Create A Rigorous Science of Social Change”.

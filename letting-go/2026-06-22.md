We can imagine the bad boy is into communism, or better, paradigmatic civilizational renewal (we'll obviously have to find better, simpler words 😉).

Also what Martin did with this series on Amazon which was there to find Finders.

That standard model version has some secret the government is trying to suppress -- combined in that case with the the batman/iron man trop of the powerful secretive super-rich figure who turns out to be on the side of good.

We (royal we here?) should be able to churn these out at a rate of knots. Still need a bit of work on the characters. That's what normally what matters, makes it a tiny bit more memorable.

I also read the The Goal recently and the approach of using a fiction structure (fairly cliched) for presenting the ideas and mathematics of process optimization was surprisingly effective, no matter how jejeune and crude the form and writing.

Maybe part of a [[Once and Future Sun]]

The heat waits. Stifling. Not even a dawn breeze. The only recompense the sight of stars. Their immensity as baffling now, as it was the first time he looked up into the heavens.

He feels the rustle of the desert, its settling as it readies for another day. His legs ache and he thinks of the ochres and reds, the way the hills of sandy rock merge into each other.

---

Swept up in past lives
Our present vanishes
Lost in seasaw of reflection
Of wandering wondering
That leads nowhere but mirrors

Here and now are forgotten
In a bustle of tomorrows
That light fools the way
To dusty death

But this is no brief candle
This is an endless future
made of the wheels turning
By a hungry, anxious mind

Fed on scraps of yearning
And remembrance
It is half won and woven
From an ever forgetting

Ay, but in that forgetfulness
There's the rub
For in those moments, there is a shing
A lighthouse through the fog

Calling us home to a
Bitter-sweet nostalgia
A train carriage in the falling twilight
A father's face turned towards the sun

Are these scraps, these fragrant fragments
The vestige of a life?
No, NO!
This is the recompense of fools.

If we turn off, or turn away
The flickering lamp, we see suddenly
The true nature of a now
The beauty of descent into an eternal present.

That something still and great
Lies beneath the surface of these lives
Something not mine or yours
But hewn from the very stone of being

Let me come to that
Let me touch it again
Let me become it
And so vanish with the world.

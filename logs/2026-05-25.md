### Migrating notes.lifeitself.org

Today we made major progress on retiring `notes.lifeitself.org` and moving its useful material into better long-term homes. Notes, essays, book reflections, operational Tao material, DDS course resources, Life Itself research pages, and related assets were reviewed, migrated, or cleaned up so the knowledge is easier to find and maintain. The migration is tracked in [life-itself/community#1347](https://github.com/life-itself/community/issues/1347).

Came across him as the only foreigner to direct a Studio Ghibli production ([[movies/The Red Turtle]]) and as someone *personally* requested by Miyazaki: 

> The film originated in 2008 when [Wild Bunch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Bunch_\(company\) "Wild Bunch (company)") co-founder Vincent Maraval visited the Japanese animation studio [Studio Ghibli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_Ghibli "Studio Ghibli") in Tokyo. Maraval met Ghibli co-founder [Hayao Miyazaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayao_Miyazaki "Hayao Miyazaki") who showed him _[Father and Daughter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Father_and_Daughter_\(film\) "Father and Daughter (film)")_ (2000), an animated short film written and directed by Dutch animator [Michaël Dudok de Wit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micha%C3%ABl_Dudok_de_Wit "Michaël Dudok de Wit"). Miyazaki told Maraval that if the studio was to ever produce a film with a foreign animator Dudok de Wit would be the one, and asked Miraval to locate him. -- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red_Turtle

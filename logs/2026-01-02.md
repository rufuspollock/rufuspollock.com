### Renamed `ideas/` to `projects/`

Have been reflecting on how to name a section that contains things I am working on. Some of which are clearly active projects while others are still exploratory or only partially formed. I initially called this section "Ideas" to reflect the early, generative stage of much of the work, but over time many of these ideas naturally develop into concrete projects.

So ... should this section be called "Projects or “Ideas" -- knowing that in both cases will have some things that are just an idea and some things that are being realized?

Choice: **Projects** because ...

"Projects" is a more conventional and legible label that matches user expectations and signals commitment and action, while still comfortably encompassing early-stage concepts. "Ideas" that contains realized work creates expectation mismatch, whereas "Projects" section with varying levels of maturity feels natural and requires no reinterpretation by the reader.

## Aside

There's a reason web pages are linear rather than a canvas (cf that article of [[Frank Chimero]] about web design). It's not about the constraint per se of the browser. It's about the linearity of thought, the need to constrain (help?) the user find a direction to travel in. Pages that have both horizontal and vertical already create confusion. Let's alone a canvas where i can wander all over the place. The canvas is great for **creating** btw ... and it's not great for presenting.

For example, take Excalidraw. I love using Excalidraw or other whiteboarding tools. And it's great for personal or group brainstorming ... and you generally can't share that result to someone else without a lot of guidance (or reworking the canvas to provide a clear path of navigation).

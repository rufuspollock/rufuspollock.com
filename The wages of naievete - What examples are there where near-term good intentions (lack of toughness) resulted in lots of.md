# The wages of naievete: What examples are there where near-term good intentions (lack of "toughness") resulted in lots of pain down the road?

I want examples where by being (foolishly) generous (naieve) at time X there was a big bad consequence at later time Y. 

This is also connects to the idea of unintended consequences, aka the wages of naivete.

Popular culture seems to value unwarranted decency over rational toughness (e.g. Harry Potter spares Draco, endless times in movies where good guy let's bad guy go initially etc). 

Furthermore, i think this lack of "rational toughness" (this unwillingness to face facts) has large negative consequences for us individually, and, most importantly, collectively (the collective problem is both bigger and worse because the costly consequences are externalised but the near-term nice payoffs are not).

### Context

population growth and population self-regulation. Sense that those who advocate for this are authoritarian or "harsh" (evidence: population charity website i looked at, delicacy with which people bring this up, "Whatever happened to Monday?"). Often those would do something about this are portrayed as authoritarian.

But what are the consequences? Unchecked population growth threatens the planet and is the greatest danger to improving the lot of the poorer members of society.

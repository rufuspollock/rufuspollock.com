# Have Kibbutz's failed and why?

Remember conversation with Paul and Adam when last in Israel and strong sense that "Kibbutz's had failed".

But did they and in what way?

Some ways:

* Most moved away from full communitarianism
* Population levels in Kibbutz declined
* Some Kibbutz closed down
* More significant kibbutz social experiments (e.g. collective raising of children) stopped

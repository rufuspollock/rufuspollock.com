## Notes and Reflections

- Positive sense that these ideas are spreading. Can sound a bit generic and age of aquarius at times but that is to be expected
    - Many of these people have been doing this decades. Sense that there is a growing critical mass that even if a minority you are now able to find others and come out in the open e.g. to mention spirituality etc (cf example of psychedelics: once something you hid, now something you can openly talk about it and acknowledge you have done)
- https://transformationscommunity.org/ - Bruce Goldstein presentation
- Lots of this stuff gets funded and/or comes out of sustainability
    - cf Hannah Gosnell talk IPBES Transformative Change Assessment
    - IPBES assessment: looking at "Underlying causes of biodiversity loss" => focus on inner dimensions ❗
    - Goal: "Human and nature live in harmony" by 2050
- You can feel the coming takeover of [[green (worldview)]]
    - Focus is mostly on nature and sustainability, on relationality etc
        - e.g. in Hannah's presentation the major focus is on building "nature connectedness"
    - Art show at beginning was all about students making eco-art
    - Much less on wisdom (including reason) or metamodernism, or emphasis on consciousness evolution
- Little direct mention or use of [[integral]] e.g. Pal Dobrin talking about how more people now touch nondual experience but they don't then change worldview. classic integral point: your experience gets interpreted through your cultural frame.

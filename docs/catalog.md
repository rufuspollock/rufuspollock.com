# Public initiative catalog

The standalone gallery is published at `/projects/catalog.html`, linked from `/projects`. Its stylesheet and screenshot/font assets are adjacent, so relative URLs work both on the website and when opened locally.

Source of truth: `rufuspollock/planning`, `portfolio/catalog-data.json` and `scripts/build-catalog.mjs`. Update and verify there, then copy `portfolio/catalog.html`, `portfolio/catalog.css` and `portfolio/catalog-assets/` into this repository's `projects/` directory. Do not copy the private planning index. Screenshot provenance and the Manrope font license travel with the assets.

The first published copy contains 18 initiatives and was copied from planning commit `3b52c60` on 26 September 2026. Website screenshots are snapshots, not live status reports. Some captures include third-party subscription/cookie overlays.

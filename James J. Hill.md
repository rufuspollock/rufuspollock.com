# James J. Hill

Stub. Canadian-American railway builder (1838–1916), "the Empire Builder". Chief of the Great Northern Railway, completed St. Paul to Seattle in 1893 without federal subsidies. Left c. $63M (very roughly $1.3–1.7B today).

Great-grandfather of [[Peter Beard]] — the source of the Beard family fortune.

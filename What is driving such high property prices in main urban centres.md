# What is driving such high property prices in main urban centres

*Context: here in Lisbon and seeing building everywhere, clear a big boom going on but what stage is it at, and is permanent or will there be a bust?*

Hypothesis: City centre prices will equalise around the world (or have already)

Drives

* Price of travel is going down leading to ability to travel more easily anywhere esp for urban elites
* Digitisation means more work from anywhere
* World wealth - people in world middle-class who want to buy property both to live in and as a store of wealth (how many)
* Proportion of people speaking english

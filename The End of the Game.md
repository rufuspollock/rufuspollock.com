# The End of the Game

By [[Peter Beard]]. First published 1965; documents the elephant, rhino and hippo die-off in Kenya and Uganda. TASCHEN edition: 304pp, hardcover in slipcase.

Photographs, diaries and collage alongside historical accounts (Roosevelt, Hemingway, Blixen). See images on [[Peter Beard]].

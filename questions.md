# Questions

> 2016-11-14: Increasingly thinking that questions are a better way to focus my energies than ideas. Ideas are often solutions searching for a problem. Questions focus us on what we want to find out, not how we want to get there. They provide a better structure — it is no coincidence that SCQA is oriented around a question. Questions also avoid getting our ego involved — asking a question has less status than having a solution.
